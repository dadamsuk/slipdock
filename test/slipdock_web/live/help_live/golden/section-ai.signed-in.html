Before you start

The AI features need a model to talk to. Each person brings their own, under Account → Preferences → AI model : either an OpenRouter key, billed to your OpenRouter account, or an OpenAI-compatible endpoint of your own (LM Studio, Ollama and the like), which keeps everything on your network.

Without one, those features stay hidden. Nothing is sent to a model unless you ask, and every answer is limited to what you're allowed to read.

Ask about this board

The Chat button in the header of every board page, and of My work, opens a conversation about what's on screen (or press a ). The model is given the board, its lists, tags and people, the cards the current view shows, the board's wiki pages, and the open card in full. An open card also has Ask AI about this card at the bottom.

Switch on Edit and a request such as "set this due next Tuesday and pick a suitable priority" becomes a list of changes. Nothing happens until you press Apply , and each change is checked against your permissions first.

Search anywhere

Search covers every board at once and matches by meaning , not exact words. "The thing that was blocked on legal" finds the card whose comment said "waiting on the contract review". It searches cards, comments, status updates, recording transcripts and wiki pages.

Each result shows the passage that matched, linked straight to that comment or section. Results update as you type. Star a query to save it, and filter to one board or include archived cards. The server keeps the search index, so you don't need your own model for this.

Ask about anywhere

Ask answers a question in plain language, using every board you can read. Unlike chat, it isn't given a page. Instead it uses tools to find what it needs: searching by meaning, counting a board's cards list by list, describing a board, listing one person's work, reading the activity log ("what changed this week?"), and reading your alerts.

It shows which searches it ran and links every card it read, so you can check its answer. It only reads; it never changes anything.

Which one to use

Looks at Gives you Can change things
Ask about this board The page you're on A conversation Yes, in Edit mode, after you approve
Search anywhere Every board you can read Cards and pages, best match first No
Ask about anywhere Every board you can read An answer, with its sources No

The toggle at the top of Search and Ask carries what you typed across, so going from "I searched" to "now ask the same thing" takes one click.
