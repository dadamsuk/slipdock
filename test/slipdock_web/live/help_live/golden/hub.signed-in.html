Help

Slipdock is a kanban board with a wiki beside it. You can share it with people, automate it with rules, ask it questions, and let AI agents work on it alongside you. Choose a section below, or go straight to one of its parts.

Boards

Boards hold lists and lists hold cards. Any card can have subcards of its own, so a board can be a plan, a backlog, a sprint or a roadmap.
